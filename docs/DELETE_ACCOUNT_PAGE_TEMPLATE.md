## Body copy (meets Play Console requirements)

**Alpha Vlogs** (and [Your developer/company name], as shown on the Google Play store listing) lets you request deletion of your account and associated data.

### How to request account and data deletion

1. **From the app:** Open Alpha Vlogs → **Profile** (or **Settings**) → **Delete account** / **Request data deletion**, and follow the in-app steps; **or**
2. **By email:** Send an email to **[your-support@email.com]** from the email address linked to your account (or include your registered mobile number). Use the subject line: **“Request to delete my Alpha Vlogs account and data”**. We will confirm and process your request.

### What we delete

When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymise:

- Your **account** (profile, login identity).
- **Personal data** we hold: name, email, mobile number, address, school/grade, profile photo.
- **User-generated content** linked to your account (e.g. uploaded activity videos), in line with our data retention policy.

### What we may keep (and for how long)

- **Backups / logs:** We may retain limited data in backups or logs for up to **[e.g. 90 days / 30 days]** after deletion for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, after which it is removed or anonymised.
- **Anonymised or aggregated data:** We may retain data that no longer identifies you (e.g. aggregated statistics) as described in our Privacy Policy.

### After deletion

You will not be able to sign in or recover the account. You can create a new account later if you wish.
