Request account and data deletion – Alpha Vlogs

Alpha Vlogs (by NSNR Technologies, as shown on the Google Play and Apple App Store listings) lets you request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to request account and data deletion

  1. From the app: Open Alpha Vlogs → Profile → Delete Account, and follow the in-app confirmation steps; or
  2. By email: Send an email to support@alphavlogs.com from the email address linked to your account (or include your registered mobile number). Use the subject line: “Request to delete my Alpha Vlogs account and data”. We will confirm and process your request.

What we delete

When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymise:

  • Your account (profile, login identity).
  • Personal data we hold: name, email, mobile number, address, school/grade, profile photo.
  • User-generated content linked to your account (e.g. uploaded activity videos), in line with our data retention policy.

What we may keep (and for how long)

  • Backups / logs: We may retain limited data in backups or logs for up to 90 days after deletion for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, after which it is removed or anonymised.
  • Anonymised or aggregated data: We may retain data that no longer identifies you (e.g. aggregated statistics) as described in our Privacy Policy.

After deletion

You will not be able to sign in or recover the account. You can create a new account later if you wish.

Contact: For questions about this process or your data, contact support@alphavlogs.com or see our Privacy Policy.
